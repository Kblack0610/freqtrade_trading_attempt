# Avenues register

This reference records the avenues considered for trader and the evidence behind each verdict. Add a row before investigating a new idea; update its verdict and revisit trigger when evidence changes. `trial` means registered research or paper observation, never permission to trade money. `open` means not yet tested, `NO-GO` rejects the stated approach, and `blocked` names a missing capability or decision.

The evidence column points to repo documents or the trader lab's `docs/` directory. Lab evidence is at `~/.notes/lab/projects/current/trader/docs/`; its source-quality labels still apply, including unchecked claims and opinions. This register summarizes those documents' research verdicts without re-verifying their market, legal or tax claims. The nightly loop's event payloads and research-sync rows link here; the evidence documents retain the measurements and methods.
