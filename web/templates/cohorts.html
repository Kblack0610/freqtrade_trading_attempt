Cohorts

Each roster row in home-config names the cohort its bot reports in and the setup overlay it runs under (an id from lab/variants.toml; base is the strategy's own settings). Every cohort is dry run: this compares ways of picking and setting up bots on live prices, and a few weeks of them is too short to judge one by. The {{ paper }} cohort is the event-contract paper league: one paper account per variant of each family in lab/markets.toml [paper], filled against the recorded books; its trades are settled and open fills.
