Strategies

Every strategy backtested on each exchange's whole price history, rebuilt nightly. Ranked by the low end of the Sharpe interval, so a strategy has to look good on its unlucky draw too. Greyed rows have a bias finding or fewer than {{ min_trades }} trades in the window. DSR is the chance the Sharpe is more than the best one luck would give among the strategy and exchange pairs tested: {{ board[0].trials_raw or 0 if board else 0 }} of them, worth {{ (board[0].trials | num(1)) if board else 0 }} independent trials once their correlation is counted. More trades is how many the low end of the per-trade interval needs before it can clear zero, if the mean and spread hold. The estimates resample the last year of daily returns: they describe that year, they do not promise the next.

Fees by venue

Strategies with at least {{ min_trades }} trades in the window and no lookahead bias. An edge counts when the low end of its 90% interval per trade is above zero. Fees are the backtest's taker fee on each side; a venue where edges exist before fees but not after is too expensive for these strategies, and more pairs there will not help. Overfit is the probability of backtest overfitting: split the window into 16 blocks, take every half as in sample, and count how often the in-sample best strategy lands below the median on the other half. Above 50%, picking the best backtest on that venue does worse than picking at random, and its promotions wait.
