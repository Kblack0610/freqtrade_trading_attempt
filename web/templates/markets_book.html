Paper league

Each account is one family variant with its own paper capital. Equity is capital, plus realized profit, plus each open position at its exit price. The watcher holds each account to the daily loss limit and runs the kill drill on it; the drawdown alert fires {{ hard_stop | pct(0) }} of capital below an account's peak, the book's hard stop.
