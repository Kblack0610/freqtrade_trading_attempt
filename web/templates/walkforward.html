Walk-forward

Does tuning help on data the tuner never saw? For each of the last whole months, hyperopt tunes the strategy on the months before it, then the month is backtested with the strategy as it is and with the tuned parameters. Pooled over every month, the interval is a 90% bootstrap of the mean profit per trade. Walk-forward efficiency is the tuned return per day out of sample over the same in sample: near 1 the tuning carries over, near 0 or below it was fitted to noise.
